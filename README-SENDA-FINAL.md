# SENDA — AI-Powered Business Assistant

SENDA is an AI-powered operating system designed for service-based businesses.

It automates customer service, appointment scheduling, confirmations, reminders and follow-up across the customer journey.

Its purpose is to improve customer experience, reduce operational workload and minimize revenue loss caused by missed appointments, delayed responses and unattended inquiries.

## Business Problem

Service-based businesses frequently lose revenue due to:

- Slow response times
- Unanswered inquiries outside business hours
- Missed appointments and no-shows
- Manual scheduling processes
- Lack of follow-up
- Fragmented customer data
- Limited operational visibility
- Inconsistent customer experience

SENDA addresses these challenges through an AI-powered system that connects customer interactions with business tools, data sources and operational workflows.

## Core Capabilities

SENDA enables businesses to:

- Provide 24/7 customer service
- Respond outside business hours
- Schedule appointments
- Confirm, cancel and reschedule bookings
- Send automated reminders
- Reduce no-show rates
- Capture customer information
- Retrieve relevant business information
- Provide personalized follow-up
- Escalate conversations to human staff when required
- Integrate with calendars, databases and operational systems
- Improve visibility into customer interactions and service performance

## MCP Integration

SENDA includes a custom **Model Context Protocol (MCP)** designed to extend the operational capabilities of the AI assistant.

The MCP connects the assistant with business tools, data sources and system functions through a structured architecture.

This allows the assistant to:

- Retrieve information
- Execute controlled actions
- Connect services
- Access contextual data
- Interact with operational tools
- Manage processes through a conversational interface

The result is an intelligent operational layer that goes beyond automated responses.

SENDA is designed to interact with the business ecosystem and perform controlled actions within defined workflows.
