<h1>SENDA — AI-Powered Business Assistant</h1> <p><strong>SENDA</strong> is an AI-powered operating system designed for service-based businesses.</p> <p>It automates customer service, appointment scheduling, confirmations, reminders and follow-up across the customer journey.</p> <p>Its purpose is to improve customer experience, reduce operational workload and minimize revenue loss caused by missed appointments, delayed responses and unattended inquiries.</p> <h2>Business Problem</h2> <p>Service-based businesses frequently lose revenue due to:</p> <ul> <li>Slow response times</li> <li>Unanswered inquiries outside business hours</li> <li>Missed appointments and no-shows</li> <li>Manual scheduling processes</li> <li>Lack of follow-up</li> <li>Fragmented customer data</li> <li>Limited operational visibility</li> <li>Inconsistent customer experience</li> </ul> <p>SENDA addresses these challenges through an AI-powered system that connects customer interactions with business tools, data sources and operational workflows.</p> <h2>Core Capabilities</h2> <p>SENDA enables businesses to:</p> <ul> <li>Provide 24/7 customer service</li> <li>Respond outside business hours</li> <li>Schedule appointments</li> <li>Confirm, cancel and reschedule bookings</li> <li>Send automated reminders</li> <li>Reduce no-show rates</li> <li>Capture customer information</li> <li>Retrieve relevant business information</li> <li>Provide personalized follow-up</li> <li>Escalate conversations to human staff when required</li> <li>Integrate with calendars, databases and operational systems</li> <li>Improve visibility into customer interactions and service performance</li> </ul> <h2>MCP Integration</h2> <p>SENDA includes a custom <strong>Model Context Protocol (MCP)</strong> designed to extend the operational capabilities of the AI assistant.</p> <p>The MCP connects the assistant with business tools, data sources and system functions through a structured architecture.</p> <p>This allows the assistant to:</p> <ul> <li>Retrieve information</li> <li>Execute controlled actions</li> <li>Connect services</li> <li>Access contextual data</li> <li>Interact with operational tools</li> <li>Manage processes through a conversational interface</li> </ul> <p>The result is an intelligent operational layer that goes beyond automated responses.</p> <p>SENDA is designed to interact with the business ecosystem and perform controlled actions within defined workflows.</p> <h2>Customer Journey</h2> <pre>Customer inquiry → AI-powered customer service → needs identification → appointment scheduling → confirmation and reminders → service delivery → personalized follow-up → data capture and continuous optimization</pre> <h2>Business Impact</h2> <p>SENDA is designed to improve measurable business outcomes, including:</p> <ul> <li>Reduced no-show rates</li> <li>Faster response times</li> <li>Increased appointment confirmations</li> <li>Better customer experience</li> <li>Improved follow-up</li> <li>Reduced manual workload</li> <li>Greater operational efficiency</li> <li>Improved customer-data visibility</li> <li>Reduced revenue loss caused by delayed responses</li> </ul> <h2>Data-Driven Implementation</h2> <p>Every implementation begins with a measurable baseline.</p> <p>Depending on the business case, SENDA can be evaluated through
